Common questions

How do I sign a document?

Open it, tap Sign, then draw, type, or photograph your signature. Tap the page to place it, drag to move, pinch to resize. Save or share from the share menu.

Can I reuse my signature?

Yes. Every signature you create is saved and appears at the top of the Sign panel. It syncs to your other devices through iCloud.

Why can't the recipient move my signature?

Exports are flattened — the signature becomes part of the page itself.

I redacted something. Is the text really gone?

In the exported file, yes: pages with redactions are converted to images, destroying the underlying text. The original file on your device is unchanged.

My edits disappeared

Edits autosave as a draft and the document shows "Draft in progress" in the library. Reopen it to continue. Drafts sync through iCloud, so check that iCloud Drive is enabled on both devices.

How do I cancel Pro?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Copybook › Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. A Lifetime purchase is not a subscription and needs no cancelling.

I paid but the app says I'm on the free tier

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases, making sure you're signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Copybook is built so that your documents never leave your control. We operate no servers and we collect no personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no account system. We cannot see your documents, your signatures, or how you use the app.

Where your documents live

  • Documents, signatures, and in-progress drafts are stored on your device.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also stored in your own private iCloud container, which syncs between your devices. This is your Apple account and your storage; we have no access to it.
  • Text recognition (OCR), signature ink extraction, and every conversion run entirely on your device.

Recordings and transcription

Recordings you make are stored on your device and in your own iCloud container, alongside the notes taken with them. Transcription uses Apple's speech recognition. Where your device and language support on-device recognition the app requires it, so the audio never leaves the device; where they do not, iOS sends the audio to Apple's speech recognition service, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We never receive the audio or the transcript.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a document or photograph a signature. Photos you pick are read only to create a PDF. Neither is uploaded anywhere.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a valid subscription exists.

Sharing

When you use the share sheet, the file goes wherever you send it — Mail, Messages, another app, or Files. That transfer is governed by whichever service you choose.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date.

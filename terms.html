1. The app

PDF SignGo lets you view, annotate, sign, convert, and export PDF documents on your own device. Viewing is free. Signing, editing, scanning, conversion, and export require a Pro subscription.

2. Subscriptions

  • Pro is offered as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, each starting with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the same price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Manage or cancel at any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancelling during the free trial avoids any charge.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under the App Store terms; we cannot issue them directly.

3. Your documents

Your documents are yours. They are stored on your device and in your own iCloud account. You are responsible for keeping backups.

4. Electronic signatures

The app applies a visual signature to a document and flattens it so it cannot easily be moved or removed. It does not apply a cryptographic (certificate-based) digital signature. Whether an electronic signature is legally binding depends on your jurisdiction and the agreement in question. Take your own legal advice for high-value or regulated documents.

5. Redaction

When you redact an area, the affected page is converted to an image on export so the covered text is destroyed in the exported file. The original file on your device is left unchanged — keep that in mind before sharing the original.

Summary

Your documents, signatures, notes and recordings are processed on your device and are never sent to us. We operate no servers and have no accounts, advertising or analytics of our own. The Google components described below handle some limited data.

What is collected, and by whom

We do not collect anything. The app uses Google's ML Kit to read text on your device. Your images and the text read from them stay on your device. ML Kit does send Google diagnostic and usage data: device model and operating system, the app's package name and version, a per-installation identifier, and performance measurements. This data is encrypted in transit. Google uses it to run and improve ML Kit, and we do not receive it.

Where your documents live

Your library is stored in the app's private storage on your device, and appears in the system Files app. Removing the app removes the library, unless you have backed it up.

If Android backup is on, the system may copy your library, up to its 25 MB limit, to your own Google account. You control this in your device settings. A backup file you make yourself goes wherever you choose to save it.

Camera and photos

The document scanner is provided by Google Play services and runs on your device. Photos you take or choose are used only for what you asked: a signature, a PDF or a lesson. We do not receive them.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording, and a notification shows while a recording runs. Recordings stay on your device unless you share them.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app only which purchases are active.

Sharing

When you share a file, it goes wherever you send it. That transfer is governed by the service you choose.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, or remove the app to delete its library. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date.
